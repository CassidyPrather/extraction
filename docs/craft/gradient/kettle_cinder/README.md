# Kettle and Cinder: the ways into hot ground, told before the line

Where a walked way goes from quiet ground (danger 0-1) into hot (2-3), the
place tells of itself before you are in it (`map_gradient::crossings`, and
Robot minds' gauge `every_quiet_to_hot_crossing_is_told_by_its_place`). A
tell counts only if it is found in the approach frame without a caption:
ahead, within 35 degrees of the way's heading, from somewhere on the last
60 m short of the line, big enough for its distance, and in sight.

In Kettle and Cinder those crossings had nothing. Each now has a wreck by
the way, short of the line, level under its foot and clear of roads and
ways (map lint): Kettle mine from the Mine yard route, Miners' Row, the
Weir cottage and the Low farm; the Charcoal burners' hut on the lodge
route; the Hunting lodge home from the quarry; Cinder's North sidings, the
works on the scout's way to the chimney and on the route to the Works
yard. A gap of quiet ground under 20 m between two hot places is no
crossing (walked hot to hot).

A placement near a way can move the way, not only the view: the first
spot for the Works yard wreck pushed the way home from Ash Row over and
its first stop lost its lamp at night (`map_night`); it went to the other
side.
