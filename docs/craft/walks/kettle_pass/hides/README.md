# Kettle's hides: the mine yard (tier 2)

Progression's cautious careers lost about half their tier-2 raids in
Kettle, on the boxcar job (a survey recorder in the boxcar by the Kettle
mine, r12-r15). What the map had to do with it, and what changed
(`before_after.jpg`, the same frames before and after).

- **The mine office** (`house_brick`, tag `mine_office`), one door,
  east. Pinned there by a drone, the raider waited five minutes, then
  went down the open slope to the boxcar past the beck site's walker,
  with nothing to get down behind, and the walker met it at (-99, -12).
  Now three boulders stand a dash apart beside that way, (-62, 33) to
  (-85, 8). The walker stays: the danger is the job's.
- **The lamp hut** (`shed`, tag `lamp_hut`), one door, the mine yard's
  roof to wait under. Its door faced the truck, the side a walker came
  up to it while the raider hid inside. It faces west now, the
  rendezvous log pile between the door and the open ground.
- **Inside the mine office** a raider couldn't reach (-45.8, 42.4): the
  mouth of the house plan's 0.9 m inner doorway, which the indoor grid
  rasterises shut in this house. Not the map's: Models widens every
  house's inner doorways to 1.0 m, with a test.

A one-door building can't be given a back door from the map (that's a
prefab's); a hide's way out is made at its one door, by where it faces
and what stands outside it.
