## Stops

### s01: 0 m, drop

At (-71.0, 198.7), 20.6 m; looking 22 deg along the way, and 328 deg to blast_furnace (72 m).

![s01 ahead](s01_ahead.jpg) ![s01 side](s01_side.jpg)

- **Vista.** The blast furnace stands over the road on the left, silos and gantry black against the haze; a scatter of boulders, houses low in the distance. The strongest opening of any valley.
- **Draws the eye.** The furnace's lamp on its chimney; a rifle-shaped bit of junk by the verge; the row of roofs ahead.
- **For.** reveal: Arrival in the valley: the furnace says what this place is before you've taken a step.

## What's wrong

- Muddy arrival (s04): the job's point is on the road beside Ash Row, which you see best after passing it (s05). The job should stand in the Row.
- A wreck in the way (s08): the route passes within 2 m of a car wreck, which fills the frame where the yard should open.
- Dead stretch (s02 to s04, 40 to 100 m): three calms, the same straight road, the same furnace in every side view, and the Row only ever in haze. The walk's first beat after the drop is the canal bridge at 160 m.
- The furnace is in every side view of the first four stops: a good landmark, but it crowds out discovery on the near side.
