# Marrow: the depot exit's way down

The depot's exit kiosk at (-138, -8) stands on a pad 5.7 m above the pump
station's flat. Its door opens north onto a drop of about 39°, and the
only walkable way off the pad was west round the creek: 187 m to the
Ranger tower. The night-walks fix showed it, once the ledger's walk
started at the site's real doors.

The fix, in `assets/maps/marrow_creek.ron`:

- a landing at the door at the pad's height (26.9 m);
- ten terraces of 0.58 m over 30 m running east, under the paths' 22%
  grade, on a line south of the pump station's door so its front stays
  as it was;
- the "Depot exit way" path over them to the Forest path.

The sim raider's route from the door to the Ranger tower is now 106 m:
down the terraces, then over the Forest path's bridge. The map lint and
the breadcrumbs are clean.
